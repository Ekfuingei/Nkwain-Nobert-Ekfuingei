About

Relationship selling with technical range.

I started on building sites in Buea, winning supply contracts from contractors and local builders who buy on reputation. That taught me the only lesson that still matters in my work: nobody signs until they believe you will still be there after the invoice.

In 2021 I trained in DevOps and cloud so I could sell infrastructure without hiding behind an engineer. At Masters Group I used that fluency to take banks, universities, and technology companies off on-prem stacks and onto AWS.

When the firm split, I took the same motion into PropTech. At Casa I sell a live marketplace — iOS, Android, Cameroon, now Nigeria. I am looking for a harder arena: an AI company that sells a product people do not yet fully understand, into buyers who do not move lightly.

MBA and BSc in Business Administration, University of Buea. I work in English and French. I will relocate.

Mar 2021 — 2024

Masters Group

Sales Lead · Cloud computing · Institutional AWS migrations

Masters Group sold cloud against on-prem habit. I was sales lead. The buyers were conservative: commercial banks, universities, and technology companies that already had rooms full of servers and people who did not want to move. I learned the stack so the technical objection died in the first meeting, then I ran the commercial process until they signed. Result: eight banks, two universities, and two technology companies adopted AWS infrastructure.
